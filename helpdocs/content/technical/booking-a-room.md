# Booking a room from its screen

Rooms with a screen outside them can be booked on the spot, without opening
Outlook. The booking goes into the room's real calendar, so it's visible to
everyone straight away.

This page describes what someone standing at the display sees, so you can answer
questions about it and recognise which reports are faults rather than the
feature working as designed. To turn booking on for a room, see
[Room and building displays](help:room-displays).

## When you can book

The **Book Now** button only appears when all of these are true:

- the room is **free right now**;
- the next booking isn't due to start within the next 15 minutes;
- the room is connected to a calendar;
- booking from the screen has been enabled for that room.

If you don't see the button, one of those isn't true. A room that's about to be
used shows as *starting soon* rather than offering a booking.

## Making a booking

1. Tap **Book Now** on the room screen.
2. Choose how long you need:
    - Use **−** and **+** to step the duration in five-minute increments. It
      starts at 15 minutes.
    - Or tap **Until next booking** to take the room right up to whoever has it
      next.
3. Tap **Confirm**.

![Booking a room from its screen](screenshot:book-now)

The screen updates immediately and the room shows as in use. The booking appears
in the room's calendar as *Adhoc Booking*.

## Limits

- Bookings run from **now** — you can't book the room for later from the screen.
  Use Outlook for that.
- The longest booking is **four hours**.
- A booking is automatically shortened so it never overlaps the next one. Asking
  for two hours when someone else has the room in 40 minutes gives you 40
  minutes.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What happened |
|---|---|
| **Room is currently in use** | Someone booked it in the moment between the screen updating and you tapping Confirm. Wait for the screen to refresh. |
| **Duration must be a multiple of 5 between 5 and 240** | The requested length wasn't valid. Use the − and + buttons. |
| **Could not reach the booking service** | The connection to Outlook failed. Nothing was booked. Try again shortly, or book in Outlook. |

Nothing is booked unless the screen confirms it. If you're unsure, check the
room's booking list on the screen.

## The status light

Some rooms have a coloured light on or beside the screen:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Green** | Free |
| **Amber** | Free, but the next booking starts within 15 minutes |
| **Red** | In use |

The light follows the same information as the screen, so it's a quick way to
check a corridor of rooms at a glance. Not every room has one — if yours doesn't,
the screen itself always shows the status.
