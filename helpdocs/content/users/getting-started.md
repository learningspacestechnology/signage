# Getting started

This page gets you signed in and oriented. It assumes someone has already
created your account — if they haven't, see
[Nobody has set my account up yet](#nobody-has-set-my-account-up-yet) at the
bottom.

## Signing in

1. Go to the address your administrator gave you. Any web address for the
   service sends you to the sign-in page.
2. Choose **Sign in with Microsoft**. Your work account is used, so there is no
   separate password to remember.
3. If Microsoft asks you to confirm your identity, complete that as normal.

![The sign-in page](screenshot:login-page)

You land on the **Overview Dashboard**, which summarises your screens, content,
playlists and schedules. See [Reading the dashboard](help:dashboard) for what each
panel means.

### Signing in with a password instead

Some accounts — usually service or test accounts — have their own password. On
the sign-in page choose **Sign in with a password instead**, then enter your
username and password. If a password sign-in has just failed, this form is shown
straight away so you can try again.

## Finding your way around

The menu down the left-hand side is grouped by what you are doing:

| Group | What lives there |
|---|---|
| **Screens & Playlists** | Content, playlists, schedules and screens — the day-to-day work |
| **Room Schedules** | Buildings, rooms and room groups for room-booking displays |
| **Task Management** | Background jobs. Usually only of interest to technical staff |
| **Users, Groups & Teams** | Account administration |
| **Help** | These pages |

Along the top you'll see which **team** you are currently working in. Everything
you see in lists is limited to that team — see [Teams](help:teams) if a colleague's
work seems to be missing.

## Your first five things to do

Work through these in order. Each links to the page that covers it properly.

1. **[Understand teams](help:teams)** — so you know why you can see some things and
   not others.
2. **[Learn the four building blocks](help:concepts)** — content, playlists,
   schedules, screens. Ten minutes well spent.
3. **[Upload some content](help:content)** — an image, a video or a web page.
4. **[Build a playlist](help:playlists)** — put your content in the order you want it
   shown.
5. **[Create a schedule and point a screen at it](help:schedules)** — decide when the
   playlist plays, then attach a screen.

## Signing out and passwords

- **Sign out** using the menu at the bottom of the left-hand sidebar. You'll get
  a confirmation page with a link to sign in again.
- **Change your password** from the same menu, under **Change password**. This
  only applies to accounts that sign in with a password — if you use
  **Sign in with Microsoft**, your password is managed by your organisation and
  this page won't help you.

## When you can't get in

### "Your account is not configured"

Your Microsoft sign-in worked, but your account hasn't been granted access to
{{ config.ADMIN_SITE_NAME }} yet. This is normal the very first time you try to
sign in.

**What to do:** contact your administrator and ask them to enable your account.
They can find you in the user list already — signing in created a record for
you — so they just need to grant access and add you to a team.

### "No team assigned"

Your account is enabled, but you aren't a member of any team yet, so there is
nothing for you to manage.

**What to do:** contact your administrator and ask to be added to the right team.
If you're not sure which team you need, tell them which screens or buildings you
look after.

### Nobody has set my account up yet

Ask your administrator to create an account for you. They'll need your work email
address, and to know which team you should belong to.

!!! note
    Both of the messages above are dead ends by design — you can't reach these
    help pages until your account has full access. If you are the administrator
    resolving one of these for somebody else, see
    **Users and teams** in the technical documentation.
