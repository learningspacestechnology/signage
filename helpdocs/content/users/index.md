# Help & Tutorials

Everything you need to run digital signage in {{ config.ADMIN_SITE_NAME }} —
putting content on screens and deciding when it plays.

New here? Read **Getting started**, then **How it all fits together**. Those two
pages take about ten minutes and will save you a lot of guesswork. After that,
dip into whichever page matches the job in front of you.

Every page is written as a set of numbered steps you can follow while you work.
If something isn't behaving as you expect, jump to
**Something isn't showing on a screen** — it is organised by symptom rather than
by feature.

!!! tip "Help follows you around"
    Most screens in {{ config.ADMIN_SITE_NAME }} show a link to the page that
    explains them, just above the list or the form. You rarely need to come back
    here to find the right page.
